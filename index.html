Instant transfer

Enter the details. Review before you send.

The Instant Transfer workflow is structured around the information required for a transfer request: recipient account number, bank name, recipient name, amount and an optional note.

Recipient account + bank Identify where the transfer request is intended to go.
Recipient name Keep the recipient information visible during review.
Amount and note Enter the amount and optionally add a description for the request.
