About

Designed around clarity.

Cash Flow Bank is a financial platform prototype focused on creating a polished account-management experience. The interface demonstrates account creation, customer dashboards, transfer workflows, cashflow analysis and customer support.

One organized dashboard.

Customers can view account information, review activity, create transfer requests, manage their profile, update their security PIN and contact support through live chat.
